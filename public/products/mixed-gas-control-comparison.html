DECISION PRINCIPLE

Choose between two product forms—not between high and low grades

COMPARISON QUESTIONS

Common product-selection misunderstandings

Should the cabinet and MSPV2_4000 be used together?

No. They are alternative mixed-gas controllers. Choose one for the project; do not arrange them as a serial mixer pair.

Is MSPV2_4000 the upgraded version of the cabinet?

No. It is a different product form for compact OEM and retrofit integration. The cabinet remains the packaged external option.

Which product gives better cutting results?

The current evidence does not support a universal winner. Cutting results depend on the complete machine, gas supply, settings, material and integration conditions.
