CABINET QUESTIONS

What the packaged form changes

Does the cabinet and MSPV2_4000 perform the same function?

Yes. Both control N₂/O₂ mixed gas. The cabinet is a packaged external system; MSPV2_4000 is a compact integration product. They are alternatives, not serial components.

Is the cabinet plug-and-play with every laser?

No. Gas sources, pressure, flow, piping, laser demand and site installation must be checked before compatibility is confirmed.

What laser powers are covered by the current evidence?

The existing cabinet documentation and site evidence span 6–60 kW applications. That is evidence coverage, not automatic compatibility with every machine in that range.
