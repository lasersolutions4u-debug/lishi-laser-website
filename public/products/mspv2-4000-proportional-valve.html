CONTROL PRINCIPLE

Closed-loop feedback adjusts the proportional valve output

Pressure and flow feedback are compared with the selected targets. The controller drives the valve mechanism to regulate gas ratio, flow and outlet pressure for the specified process.

INSTALLATION CONDITIONS

Compact hardware does not remove the need for gas-path engineering

Recommended inlet pressure is 1.5–1.6 MPa, with the N₂/O₂ inlet pressure difference kept within 100 kPa. Upstream regulation, check valves, clean gas, mounting and electrical integration remain part of the project assessment.

MSPV2_4000 QUESTIONS

What must be assessed before integration

Is MSPV2_4000 an internal part of the mixing cabinet?

No. MSPV2_4000 and the integrated cabinet are alternative products with the same core N₂/O₂ mixing and output-control function.

Do analog and NPN/PNP interfaces mean it fits every laser controller?

No. They are available interface types. Signal definitions, voltage, logic and the complete control sequence must be checked for the target machine.
